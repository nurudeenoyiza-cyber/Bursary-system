Portal Application Guide

Follow our verified roadmap to successfully prepare your documents, avoid common portal verification mistakes, and guarantee your bursary grant disbursement.

Walkthrough

Step-by-Step Portal Submission

Follow these 5 structured stages to ensure your bursary request passes automated screening and manual validation.

1

Digital Document Preparation

Scan all prerequisites before beginning the online form

Ensure your Local Government Certificate of Indigeneity, admission letter, institutional identity card, and academic results are scanned clearly. File sizes should ideally be under 300KB each in PDF or JPEG formats.

Pro Tip: Blurry or cut-off uploads result in automatic disqualification during audit rounds. Use flatbed or high-resolution mobile scanners.
2

Portal Account Registration

Access official URL: scholarshipboard.kogistate.gov.ng

Navigate to the active Bursary portal tab. Click "New Registration / Apply Now". Enter your functional email address, create a secure password, and supply your personal mobile telephone number. Confirm your activation link sent via email.

3

Indigeneity & NIN Verification

Selecting your LGA of Origin

Select your authentic Local Government Area from the 21 Kogi LGAs. Provide your 11-digit National Identification Number (NIN). Your first name, middle name, and surname must correspond with your NIN database record.

4

Academic Records & Matriculation

Institutional confirmation

Select your public institution from the verified directory. Input your exact Matriculation Number, Faculty, Department, and current Level of study. Upload your admission letter and semester statement.

5

Bank Account Match & Acknowledgement Slip

Direct payment routing

Enter your commercial Nigerian bank account details (NUBAN). The account name must exactly match the name on your student profile. Review all entries, submit, and print/save your official submission acknowledgement slip.

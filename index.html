Flagship Scheme

Undergraduate & HND/ND

₦30,000 / Session

Dedicated general bursary assistance for all verified Kogi State indigenes pursuing diploma and degree courses across accredited Nigerian universities and polytechnics.

Simplified Registration

4-Step Application Roadmap

Applying for the Kogi State bursary has been completely digitized for maximum transparency, speed, and fairness.

01

Create Student Profile

Visit scholarshipboard.kogistate.gov.ng and register your account using your active email and official phone number.

02

LGA & Indigeneity

Enter your 11-digit NIN and upload your verifiable Local Government Indigene Certificate from your Kogi LGA.

03

Upload Documents

Scan and upload your valid admission letter, school ID card, matriculation number details, and previous semester statement.

04

BVN & Direct Credit

Provide your commercial bank account matching your registered name for direct digital disbursement upon verification.

Bursary Schemes & Eligibility

Review the comprehensive eligibility criteria, monetary allowances, qualifying institutions, and documentation standards for Kogi State tertiary bursaries.

Financial Aid Breakdown

Government Approved Bursary Awards

Each scheme has been structured under the executive administration of Gov. Ahmed Usman Ododo to meet the actual financial demands of diverse disciplines.

Undergraduate & Technical

General Undergraduate & Polytechnic Bursary

For degree, Higher National Diploma (HND), Ordinary National Diploma (ND), & NCE candidates.
₦30,000
Disbursed Per Session

Key Eligibility Requirements

  • Must be a certified indigene of Kogi State (any of the 21 LGAs).
  • Full-time student in an accredited Federal or State tertiary institution.
  • Maintain satisfactory academic progress in current session.

Mandatory Documents

  • Local Government Indigene Certificate.
  • Valid Admission Letter & Student Identity Card.
  • Statement of Result (or matriculation proof for freshers).
Direct disbursement credited directly to verified bank accounts. Step-by-Step Guide
Clinical Healthcare

Medical & Dental Students Special Grant

For MBBS, BDS, Pharmacy, and specialized Clinical Health trainees.
₦75,000
Clinical Training Support

Eligibility Criteria

  • Verified Kogi State indigene enrolled in accredited College of Medicine.
  • Active enrollment in clinical year (300 level to 600 level).
  • Validated by medical students association (e.g. NIMSA Kogi Chapter).

Required Documents

  • LGA Indigene identification certificate.
  • Dean/Provost of College of Health Sciences validation letter.
  • University Student ID & Registration slips.
Special grant approved to alleviate high medical equipment and logbook costs. Check Verification Process
Executive Flagship Grant

Nigerian Law School Executive Bursary

Approved by Executive Governor Ahmed Usman Ododo for prospective Bar Part II candidates.
₦300,000
One-Off Executive Bar Subsidy

Bar Eligibility Specifics

  • Must be a bonafide Kogi indigene admitted into Nigerian Law School campuses.
  • Duly registered with National Association of Kogi State Law Students (NAKOLSON).
  • Official endorsement by Law School campus chapters.

Verification Checklist

  • Nigerian Law School (NLS) Admission Letter & ID.
  • Certificate of Indigeneity from respective Kogi LGA.
  • LL.B Degree statement of result / graduation certificate.
Provides crucial relief for Law School tuition, course materials, and dining fees. Inquire With Desk Liaison
